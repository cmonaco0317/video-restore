Video Upscaler

Pick the sites where it should turn itself on. Everywhere else, invoke it manually.

Always upscale on these sites

    Other ways to invoke it

    ⌘ ⇧ U  — toggle on the current tab. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

    Right-click a video or the page → Upscale video on this page.

    Pin the toolbar icon — click the puzzle-piece 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Video Upscaler. One click from then on, and the icon shows ON while it is active.

    Allowing a site grants this extension permission to read and change pages on that site — that is what lets it run without being asked. Remove a site at any time and the permission is revoked with it.
